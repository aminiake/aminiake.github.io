Who we are

A link between children with special needs and the wider community

In rural Kenya, a child with a disability can easily go unseen. Therapy is far away and costly, and caring for the child often falls on one parent alone.

Our mission is a community where children with special needs are accepted, and where neighbours, churches and friends help identify and meet their needs.

Too often

Hidden and left out

Children with special needs stay out of sight, and their families carry the load with little help.

The Aminia way

Seen, visited and supported

We visit families at home, build lasting relationships, meet real needs and connect them to a caring community.

How it works

From first call to lasting support

  1. Referral

    A family, school, church or community health volunteer tells us about a child who needs help.

  2. Home visit

    We meet the child with their parent or guardian present and learn what the household needs.

  3. Support plan

    Together we agree what support the child receives and who does what.

  4. Follow-up

    We visit regularly, track progress and adjust the plan as the child grows.

Accountability

Built to be trusted with your gift

Safeguarding first

Our child protection framework follows Kenyan law:

  • Children Act 2022
  • Persons with Disabilities Act 2025
  • Data Protection Act 2019

Independent board

A board of five to nine members oversees our work, led by a Director, Deputy Director, Secretary and Treasurer.

Open with donors

We will publish annual reports and financial statements. Any donor can ask how their gift was used.

Aminia KE is completing registration with Kenya's Public Benefit Organisations Regulatory Authority under the PBO Act 2013.

Questions donors ask

Frequently asked questions

Can I give from outside Kenya?

Yes. Click Donate, then “Give online” to pay by card or PayPal from anywhere in the world through our M-Changa fundraiser.

How do I give by M-Pesa?

Go to M-Pesa, choose Lipa na M-Pesa, then Pay Bill. Enter business number 891300 and account number 74174, then the amount. You can also dial *483*57*74174#.

Where does my money go?

Gifts pay for basic needs, food baskets, home visits and help reaching therapy and school for the children and families we walk with. We will report how funds were used in our annual report.

Who do you support?

Children aged 5–17 with special needs, such as cerebral palsy, autism and spinal conditions, and the parents and guardians who care for them, mostly in rural areas of Kenya.

Is Aminia a faith-based organisation?

Our team is motivated by Christian faith, and we pray with families who welcome it. Support is given to every family we serve regardless of their beliefs.

Can I give food or items instead of money?

Yes. Through Jazakikapu you can give single food items or a full basket at Christmas. Message us on WhatsApp to arrange it.

How do you protect the children you serve?

Everyone who works with children goes through safeguarding checks, home visits follow set procedures, and personal information is handled under Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019.
